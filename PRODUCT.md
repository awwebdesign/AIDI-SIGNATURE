# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Ambitious pigeon fanciers in Belgium and the Netherlands: hobbyists who already race seriously and want to move up. They know their own loft intimately, argue about feed on forums and at the club, and are sceptical of marketing claims but genuinely interested in the science. They arrive mid-season with a specific question (which mix for Sunday's 500km, what to give on return), or in winter while planning next year's programme.

A secondary audience reads over their shoulder: top lofts already chasing national ace titles, and the international distributors in eleven countries who use the site as a credibility document. The champions' results are what convince the ambitious fancier, so both audiences are served by the same proof.

## Product Purpose

AIDI is the feed and supplement range built by Team Noël-Willockx. Eddy Noël brings 45 years as a specialist pigeon nutritionist, and Ivan Willockx, ex-professional footballer turned pigeon broker. Ten racing and breeding mixes, fourteen supplements, two equipment lines, sold through roughly thirty-five Belgian shops and distributors in the Netherlands, Germany, UK, France, Italy, Hungary, Croatia, USA, Czechia and Poland.

The site exists to move a fancier from curiosity to a considered choice of product, and then to a shop that stocks it. Success is a visitor who understands *which* AIDI product fits their situation and why, not one who has merely read that AIDI is good.

## Positioning

AIDI publishes the numbers nobody else in pigeon sport publishes: measured fat, protein, absorbable protein, carbohydrate, energy and omega ratio for every mix. Nutrition treated as sports science, for an animal that is an athlete.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: explore the product range, reaching a specific product and understand where it belongs in the season.
- Secondary CTA: find a stockist (thirty-five Belgian shops, distributors in ten more countries), or call Eddy and Ivan directly.
- The line a visitor remembers after ten seconds: every AIDI mix comes with its numbers.
- Belief ladder: (1) a racing pigeon is an athlete and diet decides performance; (2) AIDI knows this more precisely than the competition, and proves it with published analytical values; (3) the range is a system rather than a shelf, with a right product for this week of the season and this distance; (4) the results of lofts using it are real and national; (5) I can get it near me.
- Proof on hand: KBDB National Ace Pigeons over many years (1st Nat. Ace Long Distance Old Birds 2022 – Roziers-Xiang; 1st Nat. Ace GMD Young Birds 2021; 1st Nat. Brive 3,755 birds 2020; 1st Nat. Ace Middle Distance Young Birds 2010). 2022 highlights including 1st Nat. Argenton against 5,166 birds, 15× top-100 national. Fourteen published feeding schedules. Full analytical data per product. Team photo, product photography for 25 of 26 products, dealer list across eleven countries.

## Brand Personality

Precise, measured, expert. The voice of a sports nutritionist, not a salesman: it explains mechanisms (glycogen, absorbable protein, omega ratio, gut flora) and trusts the reader to follow. AIDI's own copy already calls pigeons "onze atleten"; the site should take that literally rather than treat it as a flourish. Confident without shouting. Belgian, understated, evidence-first. Warmth comes from the two men and their forty-five years, never from decoration.

## Anti-references

- The current aidi.be: teal Bootstrap chrome, Varela Round, flag-icon language switcher, thirty products dumped into a nav dropdown, proof buried three clicks deep.
- Agricultural feed-store catalogues: barn photography, green-and-brown palettes, undesigned spec tables.
- Nutraceutical webshops: pill-bottle grids, urgency badges, clinical sterility.
- Generic AI landing pages: gradient text, glassmorphism, identical three-icon feature cards, an uppercase tracked eyebrow above every section, hero-metric templates.

## Design Principles

**The data is the design.** The analytical values are AIDI's real differentiator, so they become the visual system (comparable, plottable, scannable) rather than a paragraph at the bottom of a page.

**Show the season, not the shelf.** A fancier thinks in phases (breeding, racing, moult, winter rest) and distances. Product navigation should follow that model, not alphabetical order.

**Proof before claims.** National ace titles and race results are load-bearing content, placed where a sceptic meets them early, not archived on a results page.

**Trust the reader.** Do not simplify the science away. Explaining absorbable protein properly is what separates AIDI from a feed merchant.

**Every product ends somewhere real.** No dead end: each product leads to a stockist, a feeding schedule, or Eddy and Ivan's phone numbers.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Body text ≥4.5:1, large text ≥3:1, visible focus on every interactive element, full keyboard operation of the language switcher, product filters and detail panels. Five languages (NL/FR/EN/DE/ZH) with correct `lang` attributes so screen readers switch pronunciation. Motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`. Data must never be conveyed by colour alone. Every chart segment carries a label and a value. The audience skews older, so generous type sizes and touch targets ≥44px.
